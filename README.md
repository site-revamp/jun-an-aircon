# Jun An Aircon Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

Jun An Aircon Service Pte. Ltd. provides aircon installation, servicing, maintenance, and repairs for homes and businesses in Singapore. The proposed homepage helps visitors identify the right service, understand pricing, and request a quotation.

## Main Improvements

1. **Organise services by customer need.** The original homepage makes visitors search across sections to identify the right option. The proposed layout gives residential servicing, commercial aircon work, installation, and specialist systems such as VRV/VRF and cassette units distinct sections.
2. **Give experience and credentials clear context.** The proposed homepage would attribute the advertised experience to the team’s industry background once confirmed and state the company’s 2023 incorporation separately. It would give verified credentials, genuine customer feedback, and actual project photos more prominence.
3. **Connect pricing to enquiries.** The proposed pricing layout would show what each service includes and which charges may be extra. Service-specific enquiry buttons, a short quotation form, and persistent mobile Call and WhatsApp actions would shorten the path from comparing options to getting in touch.

## Design Improvements

Distinct headings would make service categories easier to scan. A consistent pricing layout would keep inclusions, extra charges, and enquiry actions together so visitors can compare services before contacting Jun An.
